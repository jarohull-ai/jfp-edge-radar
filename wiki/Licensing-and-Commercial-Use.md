## 🛡️ The Dual-Tier Fair Defensive Model

Critical cybersecurity defenses against zero-day exploits and autonomous AI agent botnets must be accessible to everyone to safeguard the open Internet. However, multi-billion-dollar enterprise platforms monetizing massive traffic should contribute to sustainable defense maintenance.

### 1. Free Defensive Community Tier (< $10,000,000 USD Annual Revenue)
* **Who is covered:**
  * All private individuals, security researchers, educators, and students.
  * All non-profit, educational, and public-interest organizations.
  * Any commercial startup, company, or business with annual gross revenue **under \$10,000,000 USD**.
* **Rights:** 100% free of charge to use, inspect, modify, compile, deploy in production, and run on any number of servers without license fees or telemetry.

### 2. Commercial Enterprise Tier (>= $10,000,000 USD Annual Revenue)
* **Who is covered:**
  * Any commercial corporation, enterprise, financial institution, or cloud infrastructure provider with aggregate annual gross revenues of **\$10,000,000 USD or higher**.
* **Requirements:** Requires a commercial enterprise license agreement from JARO before running or embedding in commercial production.
